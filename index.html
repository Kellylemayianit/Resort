The fire

It begins at the grill

Meat is roasted slowly over charcoal and turned by hand until it's ready to share — nyama choma served with ugali, kachumbari and sukuma wiki, the way it's meant to be eaten.

The table

Plates made for sharing

Fried tilapia, beef, fresh greens and soft ugali arrive together, with a cold drink from the bar. Nothing rushed, nothing fussy — a long lunch after a game drive, or an evening with friends.

The setting

Kimana, Kajiado County

The resort sits in Kimana, on the savannah corridor between Amboseli National Park and Mount Kilimanjaro — one of East Africa's most recognisable views of the mountain, and a well-known gateway town for travellers moving between Amboseli and the Tsavo parks.
